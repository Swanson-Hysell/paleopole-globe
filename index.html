About these data

How to use PaleopoleGlobe

Each continent carries its paleomagnetic poles: where the north pole appears to have been, seen from that continent, at each age. Poles are glued to their plate, so when you move a plate its poles move with it.

Reconstructing a continent

  1. Click Part B or Part C under Set up for to show the continents you need.
  2. Choose the focus age under Paleomagnetic poles. That age’s pole is drawn large and labelled.
  3. Drag the large pole marker onto the North Pole crosshair. The continent moves with it, and the pole snaps on when it is within 2°.
  4. The continent is now at its paleolatitude and in its ancient orientation for that age. Its longitude is still unknown.
